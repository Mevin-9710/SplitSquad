The best friend groups don't just have fun together — they're financially organized. Here are 5 habits that separate chaotic groups from smooth-running squads.

## 1. Weekly Settlement Night

Pick one night a week (Sunday works best) to settle all outstanding expenses. No more than 7 days of tracking.

**Why it works**: Small amounts don't pile up. Everyone remembers what they spent. No awkward "hey, remember that dinner from 3 weeks ago?"

  Use SplitSquad's recurring split feature for weekly automatic reminders. Everyone gets a WhatsApp message on Sunday evening with their outstanding balance.
</HighlightBox>

## 2. The Treasurer Role

Rotate the "squad treasurer" monthly. This person is responsible for:
- Collecting money for shared expenses
- Creating splits in SplitSquad
- Sending reminders
- Reconciling at month end

## 3. The 24-Hour Rule

Settle any single expense over ₹5,000 within 24 hours. Big amounts need quick resolution to prevent cash flow issues for the person who paid.

## 4. Shared Savings Goals

Groups that save together, stay together. Common shared goals:
- **Trip fund**: ₹500/month per person → enough for a Goa trip in 6 months
- **Emergency food fund**: ₹200/month per person → covers group Zomato when someone's short
- **Group gift fund**: ₹100/month per person → birthday gifts sorted

## 5. The Pre-Trip Agreement

Before any group trip, agree in writing:
- Total budget range
- How shared costs will be split
- Who handles payments
- Settlement timeline
