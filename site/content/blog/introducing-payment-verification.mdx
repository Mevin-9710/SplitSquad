Today, we're launching one of our most requested features: **Public Payment Verification**.

Here's the problem it solves: how many times has someone in your group claimed they paid — but the rest of you aren't sure? "Bro I already paid" is the most common phrase in Indian group chats after "where to eat."

## How It Works

When you create a split in SplitSquad, each participant now gets a unique verification link sent via WhatsApp.

## Key Features

### Single-Use Codes
Each verification link expires after one use. No reusing old links to claim you paid.

### No Account Required
Your friends don't need to sign up. They click the link, confirm their payment, and done.

### Real-Time Dashboard
You can see exactly who has verified and who hasn't — no more checking your bank app 5 times.
