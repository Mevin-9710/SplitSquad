Between hostel fees, shared subscriptions, group projects, and weekend outings — college expenses add up fast. And tracking who paid for what is the last thing you want to deal with between exams.

## The College Expense Problem

- **Group meals**: Hostel mess is covered, but outside food needs tracking
- **Trip expenses**: That weekend trip to the hills
- **Stationery & supplies**: Group project materials
- **Late-night Zomato orders**: The classic shared expense

## The 3 Subscriptions Rule

Most college friend groups share 3-4 subscriptions. The fairest way:

1. One person pays for each subscription
2. Everyone splits the total equally
3. Use SplitSquad to create a recurring monthly split
