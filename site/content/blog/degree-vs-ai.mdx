Here's the uncomfortable truth no one tells you in college orientation:

> 40% of graduates aged 15–25 are jobless.

That's the headline from the Azim Premji University State of Working India 2026 report. Not a recession. Not a bad year. A structural shift in what employers want vs. what colleges deliver.

And AI is accelerating it.

## Why Your Degree Isn't Enough Anymore

### The Experience Paradox

72% of entry-level jobs now demand prior experience — but only 20% of students have access to paid internships.

You can't get a job without experience. You can't get experience without a job. The system is designed to keep you out.

### The AI Disruption

235 million monthly AI searches in India — up 154% YoY. 80% of students already use GenAI for job applications. Employers are redefining "qualified":

- AI fluency is now a non-negotiable gatekeeper
- 64% of HR leaders define premium talent by AI/ML, Data, Cloud, and Cybersecurity skills — not college pedigree
- "Blue-collar tech" workers (trade skills + digital literacy) are the fastest-growing segment

<HighlightBox variant="warning" title="The Brutal Metric">
  30% of MBA graduates earn below ₹10 LPA. 39% of engineering graduates earn below ₹7 LPA. The degree premium is compressing across the board (Unstop Talent Report 2026).
</HighlightBox>

## What Actually Gets You Hired

The old formula (degree → job → success) is being replaced by:

**Skills + Portfolio + Communication > Degree + GPA + College Name**

## The 90-Day Skill Building Plan

**Month 1 — Pick One:**
- AI basics: Learn to use ChatGPT/Claude effectively for real tasks
- Content: Write 15 LinkedIn posts in your target field
- Design: Complete a Figma or Canva certification

**Month 2 — Build Evidence:**
- [**Freelance**](/blog/side-hustle-era) 3 small projects (even unpaid for portfolio)
- Create a simple portfolio website (10 minutes on Notion)
- Solve 5 real problems using AI tools

**Month 3 — Get Visible:**
- Apply to 50 jobs (not 5)
- Message 20 people in your target industry on LinkedIn
- Share your work publicly

<HighlightBox variant="tip" title="The Rule">
  Your first job isn't about maximising income. 90% of students would take a lower salary for better learning opportunities (Unstop 2026). Prioritise skill-building over starting salary in year one.
</HighlightBox>
