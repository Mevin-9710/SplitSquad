Your monthly allowance lands in your UPI. ₹8,000 if you're frugal, ₹15,000 if you're lucky.

By the time you've paid mess fees, recharged your phone, and bought that one textbook the professor actually uses — you're down to ₹3,000 and it's only the 10th.

College life in India is a crash course in adulting, and the hardest lesson isn't coursework — it's [**managing money with roommates**](/blog/paying-30k-for-a-box) when everyone's running thin margins.

## The Three Rules of Hostel Finance

### Rule 1: Settle Weekly, Not at Semester End

This is the single most important habit for student groups. Large accumulated balances are stressful — especially on tight budgets where ₹3,000 is not pocket change.

Small weekly settlements feel completely neutral. A quick UPI round on Sunday, and everyone's clean.

### Rule 2: Separate Room Group from Friend Group

Hostel life has two expense circles:
- **Room group** (2-3 people): shared supplies, electricity, common snacks
- **Friend group** (4-8 people): Zomato runs, movie tickets, chai breaks

Keep them separate. Priya owing you ₹120 for a room extension cord shouldn't get mixed up with the ₹720 Zomato biryani night.

### Rule 3: Pay for What You Actually Eat

The #1 mess conflict? People eating different numbers of meals but splitting equally.

<HighlightBox variant="tip" title="The Golden Rule">
  Transfer your savings first, not last. When your money arrives, move ₹1,000–₹2,000 to a separate account immediately. If you save what's left after spending, there will never be anything left.
</HighlightBox>

## 5 Money Mistakes That Ruin Friendships

1. **"I'll transfer tomorrow"** — Tomorrow never comes. Log expenses immediately.

2. **Forgetting small amounts** — ₹50 chai runs add up to ₹1,500/month. Log everything.

3. **Mixing personal and shared** — Your protein powder is not a shared expense. Neither is that fancy cheese.

4. **Asking someone to UPI ₹27** — Set a carry-forward threshold (₹200). Don't annoy your friends over chai money.

5. **Waiting till month-end to settle** — ₹6,400 surprise bills damage friendships. Weekly settlements save relationships.
