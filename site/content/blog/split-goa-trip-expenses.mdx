Goa trips with friends are legendary. The beaches, the parties, the late-night shack dinners — and the inevitable "who owes what" conversation on the ride back.

<HighlightBox variant="tip">
  The best Goa trips are planned with expense splitting in mind from day one. A 5-minute conversation at the start saves 5 hours of math at the end.
</HighlightBox>

## Pre-Trip: Set the Ground Rules

Before anyone books anything, agree on these three things:

1. **Who's the trip treasurer?** One person manages the SplitSquad account
2. **What's the budget range?** Luxury vs budget determines everything
3. **How will shared costs be split?** Equal or by usage

## The Goa Trip Expense Framework

### Accommodation

Most groups split this equally — everyone shares the same space. However, if someone gets the master bedroom with an attached bathroom, it's fair to charge them a premium.

### Food & Drinks

The easiest approach: one person pays for each meal, and you track it in SplitSquad. At the end of the trip, settle the difference.

### Activities & Transport

Bike rentals, water sports, club entries — these are per-person expenses. Track them individually.

## Pro Tips

- **Use a shared UPI ID** for the trip — one person collects and pays everything
- **Take photos of bills** — upload them to SplitSquad for transparency
- **Settle every 2 days** — don't wait until the trip ends
- **Include a contingency fund** — ₹2,000 per person for unexpected expenses
