## The 3 Big Differences

### 1. UPI Integration

Splitwise was designed for credit cards and bank transfers. SplitSquad was built from the ground up for UPI — India's preferred payment method.

<HighlightBox variant="info" title="Why This Matters">
  Over 10 billion UPI transactions happen monthly in India. Your expense splitting tool should speak UPI natively — not treat it as an afterthought.
</HighlightBox>

### 2. Payment Verification

This is Splitwise's biggest gap. Splitwise tracks who owes what, but it can't verify that someone actually paid. The result? "I already paid" debates that never end.

SplitSquad's verification links are single-use and public — once someone confirms, everyone sees it. No more fake screenshots.

### 3. No Signup Required

Everyone needs a Splitwise account to participate. With SplitSquad, you create the account — your friends just tap a WhatsApp link.

## Who Should Use Each?

### Choose Splitwise if:
- You're outside India (it works better internationally)
- Your group already uses it and is happy
- You need native mobile apps (iOS/Android)
- You don't care about payment verification

### Choose SplitSquad if:
- You're in India and use UPI
- You want WhatsApp-native reminders
- You want to eliminate "I already paid" debates
- Your friends are tired of making accounts
- You want the free beta access
