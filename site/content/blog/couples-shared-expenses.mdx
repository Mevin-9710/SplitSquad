Money conversations are unavoidable in relationships. Whether you're dating, living together, or married — tracking shared expenses shouldn't cause friction.

## The Three Approaches

### 1. Proportional Split (Most Popular)
Each person contributes based on their income ratio. If one earns ₹80K and the other earns ₹50K, the higher earner pays 62% of shared expenses.

### 2. Equal Split
Everything is 50-50. Simple but can feel unfair if incomes are vastly different.

### 3. Shared Account + Personal Accounts
Each month, both deposit a fixed amount into a joint account for shared expenses. Personal spending stays separate.

## What Counts as Shared?

Most couples share:
- Rent/mortgage
- Utilities
- Groceries
- Eating out together
- Travel together
- Household supplies

<HighlightBox variant="tip">
  The key to avoiding arguments is deciding upfront what's shared and what's personal. Individual clothes, hobbies, and eating out without your partner — those are personal expenses.
</HighlightBox>
